Mentorship is a central part of my academic work. I have mentored trainees and research staff in computational biology, machine learning, bioinformatics, data visualization, and translational AI projects.

My mentoring style emphasizes clear research questions, reproducible analysis, careful evaluation, and clinically meaningful interpretation. I enjoy helping trainees move from exploratory analyses to rigorous manuscripts, presentations, and reusable software workflows.

## Areas where I mentor trainees

- Machine learning for clinical and omics datasets
- Bioinformatics analysis of high-throughput sequencing data
- Data visualization and scientific communication
- Reproducible Python and R workflows
- Manuscript development and conference presentations
